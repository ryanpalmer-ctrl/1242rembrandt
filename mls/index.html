The Build

Built with the good stuff.

Built in 2023 with the kind of materials most builders save for the model home. You can see it from the street.

Why it matters

Sierra Pacific windows and Sightline steel doors are what you find on the best custom homes in the country. They seal tighter, look sharper and hold up longer, and the black frames give the house its clean, modern look.
